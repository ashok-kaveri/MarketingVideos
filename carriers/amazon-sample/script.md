## intro
In this video, I'll show you how to print Amazon Shipping labels on Shopify, one at a time or in bulk, and fulfil and track your orders, all from one app.

## single-label
Open an order to review the details, such as the shipping address, packaging details and shipping service. Once you've reviewed everything, click Generate Label.

## bulk-card
Shipping in bulk?

## bulk-labels
Just select multiple orders, click Generate Labels, and the labels are created for all of them at once.

## fulfil
Once your labels are printed and on your packages, click Mark as Fulfilled. The tracking details sync to your Shopify orders, and your customers get them by email.

## tracking-dashboard
You can also follow the live status of every Amazon Shipping order from the app's Tracking tab.
