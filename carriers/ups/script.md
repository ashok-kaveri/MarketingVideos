# UPS — narration
Source: marketing-approved script (`reference/ups/script.txt`), split per scene.

## hook (silent)

## intro
In this video, I'll show you how to automate your UPS shipping on Shopify, from displaying real-time shipping rates at checkout to generating shipping labels, scheduling pickups, and tracking orders — all from one app.

## install
First, install the PH MultiCarrier Shipping Label app from the Shopify App Store.

## address-menu
Once it's installed, the app automatically sets your Shopify store address as your shipping origin.

## address-form
You can edit this address

## address-add
or add more shipping locations if you need to.

## carriers-tab
Next, it's time to connect your UPS account. To do that, go to the Carriers tab in the app's main menu.

## ups-connect
Select UPS and click on Add Account. Under Login to Existing UPS Account, click Login. You'll be redirected to the UPS registration page. Enter your UPS account number and click Connect to Your UPS Account. Next, you'll be taken to the UPS profile login page. Enter your UPS Profile email or username, accept the terms and conditions, and click Continue. Then enter your password and click Log In. Click Submit, and you'll see a registration successful message confirming that your UPS account is connected to the app.

## packaging-menu
Now, let's see how real-time UPS shipping rates are displayed to buyers at checkout. But first, let's set up a few things that make those rates accurate. It all starts with the right packaging.

## packaging-weight
Shipping light items? Go weight-based, and rates are calculated from the product's weight.

## packaging-box
Shipping bulkier boxes? Go box-based, and rates factor in both weight and dimensions, so you never undercharge for space.

## packaging-options
But that's not all. The app offers several more packaging options, so you can choose the one that fits your store best.

## ups-carrier-menu
Prefer UPS's own packaging instead?

## ups-boxes
You can select UPS boxes too from the UPS Carrier details.

## products-menu
Add your product dimensions

## product-dimensions
in the app's Products section.

## default-dimensions
If any product is missing its weight or dimensions, the app falls back to default values, so your rates keep working at checkout.

## services-menu
Next, the UPS services.

## services-list
By default, the app enables all UPS services for your Shopify orders, but you can pick just the ones you prefer, for both checkout rates and label printing.

## negotiated-surepost
If you have UPS negotiated rates on your account, you can enable those too. You can also turn on UPS Ground Saver, formerly known as SurePost, an affordable option for lightweight residential shipments.

## special-services
Want extra protection? Add shipping insurance and signature confirmation, and enable them for both checkout rates and label printing in just a few clicks.

## checkout
With everything set up, let's head to the Shopify checkout page. When buyers enter their shipping address, they'll see the available UPS shipping rates along with estimated delivery dates. These rates are calculated based on the product's weight, dimensions, and the shipping origin and destination.

## single-label
Next, I'll show you how to print UPS shipping labels on Shopify. Open an order to review the details such as the shipping address, packaging details, shipping method, shipping cost and more. Once you've reviewed everything, click Generate Label. To print the label, click on the Print Documents dropdown and select Label.

## edit-card
Need to make changes before generating the label?

## edit-before-label
You can edit the shipping address from the order's Recipient section and update package details like the box, weight, or dimensions from the packaging block. Once you've made the changes, follow the same process to generate the shipping label.

## bulk-card
Shipping in bulk?

## bulk-labels
Just select multiple orders, click Generate Labels, and print them all at once.

## intl-card
Shipping internationally?

## intl-products-menu
First, make sure your products have their customs information added, so duties and taxes are calculated accurately.

## customs-info
Add three key details: the HS code, the country of manufacture, and the product description.

## csv-import-export
You can fill these in product by product, or export a CSV, update everything in bulk, and import it back.

## duties-payer
You can also choose who pays the duties and taxes.

## print-settings-menu
Then, in the print settings,

## print-settings
make sure the commercial invoice and any other required customs documents are enabled.

## paperless
The app also supports Paperless Invoice, so your shipment data is sent to customs electronically.

## intl-label
Once that's in place, generate the label as usual, and the app creates a commercial invoice along with the shipping label for customs clearance.

## label-ready
Once the labels are printed, go ahead and paste them on your packages.

## request-pickup
Then, select all the orders that are ready for pickup in the Orders tab and click Request Pickup. UPS will collect your packages from your location, and you can check the status anytime in the Pickup tab. If you've already opted for daily pickups, you can skip this step.

## manifest-list
The app also lets you download the manifest

## manifest-preview
that the UPS pickup agent needs.

## fulfil
Once your packages are picked up, go ahead and mark the orders as fulfilled.

## tracking-sync
This will automatically sync the tracking details to your Shopify orders and send an email to your customers with the tracking number, allowing them to track their shipments.

## tracking-dashboard
You can also monitor all your shipments from the app's built-in tracking dashboard.

## recap
That's how you can automate your UPS shipping on Shopify with the PH MultiCarrier Shipping Label app.
